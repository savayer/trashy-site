Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Trashy is a photo cleaning app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data we collect

None. Trashy does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and it makes no network requests.

Photo library access

Trashy asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos one at a time and delete the ones you choose. Your photos are read on your device only and never leave it.

Photos are deleted only after you tap Delete and confirm the iOS system dialog. iOS moves them to Recently Deleted, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

Data stored on your device

Trashy keeps a small list on your device of which photos you have already reviewed and which are in the in-app trash, so you can continue where you left off. This list stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Trashy does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
