Trashy

Swipe to delete photos

Trashy shows your photos one at a time. Swipe left to put a photo in the trash, swipe right to keep it. Nothing is deleted until you open the trash, tap Delete and confirm.

Questions

Where do deleted photos go?

To the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. You can recover them there for up to 30 days.

I swiped the wrong way. How do I fix it?

Tap the Undo button. If the photo is already in the trash, open the trash and tap the photo to keep it.

Does Trashy upload my photos?

No. Your photos never leave your phone. See the privacy policy.

How do I go through the photos I kept again?

When you reach the end of your library, tap Review again.
